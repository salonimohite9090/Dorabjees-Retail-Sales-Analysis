# Dorabjee's Retail Sales Analysis | MySQL

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data for Dorabjee's using MySQL. The objective is to examine sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, store performance, revenue, and profitability through SQL queries.

The project demonstrates relational database design and the application of SQL concepts to solve business-oriented analytical questions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue and profit.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Evaluate store-wise sales performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Examine monthly revenue trends.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts, transactions, and revenue.
- Identify profitable brands and product categories.

## Database Design

The project uses a relational database named `dorabjees_sales_analysis` with four tables:

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| Customers | Customer details, customer types, and locations |
| Products | Product names, brands, categories, selling prices, and cost prices |
| Stores | Store names and location details |
| Sales | Sales transactions, quantities, discounts, revenue, costs, and profit |

Primary keys and foreign keys establish relationships between the tables.

## Business Questions & Analysis

The project includes 17 business-oriented SQL queries addressing:

1. Total sales revenue.
2. Total profit.
3. Most frequently used payment methods.
4. Revenue generated by product categories.
5. Store performance and transaction ranking.
6. Revenue and profit by product category.
7. Monthly revenue trends.
8. Products generating below-average revenue.
9. Revenue by store and product category.
10. Average order value by customer type.
11. Top five customers by total spending.
12. Top five products by profit.
13. Discount-level analysis.
14. Profit ranking by product category.
15. Top five sales dates by revenue.
16. Brand-wise revenue and profit.
17. Top five products by quantity sold.
